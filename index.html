How to use Tech Explorers

Use a desktop or laptop computer with a keyboard and a mouse, and Chrome if you can. Phones and tablets may not work well. Choose a year, then choose a level tab (Beginner, Medium, Advanced or Advanced Pro) or the Skill Games tab, and begin with the game marked Start here. Each game has a guide to follow, so just play and collect stars. Use the pause, music and home buttons at the top, and press F11 for full screen. The arrow-key games also work with a Makey Makey: plug it in, clip the Earth lead to the player, and clip Left, Up, Down, Right and Space to fruit or foil. Progress is kept on the computer for about 3 hours, so every level stays open and a certificate comes when all the levels of a game are done. Print certificates on A4 paper.

About Tech Explorers

Why and how it was made

Tech Explorers began in a school computer club, as a friendly, free place for learners to build their computer skills, from the mouse and the keyboard to typing and beyond. It runs in the browser with no sign-in and no data collected. The ideas, the testing with real learners and every decision came from a teacher, and the code was written with an AI assistant (Claude) from those instructions. It has been tested and works well, but it is not perfect and it will keep growing. Feedback is very welcome.

Disclaimer

Tech Explorers is free to use for education and is provided as it is. It has been tested, but it may still contain mistakes. A teacher or parent should supervise young learners. It is made for a computer with a keyboard and mouse, so phones and tablets may not work as expected.
