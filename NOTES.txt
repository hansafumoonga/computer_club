COMPUTER CLUB & KEYBOARD SKILLS SUITE - teacher notes (version 2)
=================================================================

HOW TO USE
  Open index.html (double-click), or upload this whole folder to GitHub Pages as-is
  (index.html + the css and js folders; the old jump-game/shoot-game/memory-game files and files.zip are not used).
  No server, no install, no network calls, nothing is saved anywhere.

COMMANDO RUN UPGRADE (version 5)
  - Stages are about 20% longer (8,400 to 18,000 pixels).
  - New big enemies: a GIANT ROBOT that slams the ground (and sends a wave) if you get near, a TANK that lobs bombs that leave fire patches,
    a bomber HELICOPTER that hovers over you dropping bombs, and a CHARGING BEAST that crouches then rushes you (jump over it!).
  - Old enemies are more aggressive: walkers lunge, flyers swoop down at you, hoppers hop faster, gunners fire bursts. They also look nastier
    (horns, spikes, bat wings) and walkers change colour with the scenery. Every stage's half-way mini-boss is now an armoured guard, a giant
    robot or a tank in turn.
  - Bosses are bigger (x1.2), glow, and fight in 3 PHASES (the health bar shows the two marks). At 2/3 health they add new attacks, at 1/3 they
    get angry (red aura, embers, faster). New attacks: summon helpers, lunge at you, lobbed bombs that leave fire, a fast barrage of shots,
    and a LASER ACROSS CHEST HEIGHT - press Down to DUCK under it (or jump). Dragon breathes fire, UFO shines a beam, etc.
  - The nastier boss moves only appear from later stages (MOVE_MIN in js/commando.js), so stages 1 and 2 stay friendly.
  - When the boss arrives a friendly invisible wall keeps you in the left part of the arena.
  - Boss hit points: BOSS_HP_BASE / BOSS_HP_PER_STAGE. Boss moves per creature: BOSS_DEF.

MUSIC (version 4)
  Every game has its own synthesized background tune (js/music.js, no audio files): menu (bouncy), Jump Over (bright running chiptune,
  speeds up each level), Star Blaster (space arpeggios in a different key per level + a faster boss tune), Commando Run (heavy action riffs,
  a different key every stage + a darker, faster boss tune when the big creature arrives), Pattern Pop (quiet and playful so the pad tones
  stand out), Fruit Slice (relaxed marimba), Word Hunt (calm thinking music). The Piano has no background music - the child is the music.
  A music button in every top bar turns it on/off. Music gets quieter automatically while a menu / level-complete screen is showing.
  To change a tune edit TRACKS in js/music.js (bpm = speed, key, vol = loudness, and the chord / bass / drum / melody patterns).

WHAT CHANGED IN VERSION 3
  - New landing page: keycap logo, a mini keyboard for Session 1 and a Makey Makey board (pads + fruit) for Session 2.
  - Commando Run is longer (stages are 7,000 to 15,000 pixels long) and busier from the first stage: packs of enemies rushing in, a half-way
    mini-boss (an extra-big armoured guard) in every stage, long chasms crossed on stepping platforms, 3 checkpoint flags, weather and
    richer scenery, screen shake, muzzle flash and dust.
  - Fall off a platform or into a pit: you lose one heart and a friendly bubble floats you back to the last safe ground, still in the stage.
  - The bosses and enemies now look fierce (angry eyes, fangs, horns, spikes) - still cartoon, never gory. The lion was redone first.
  - Keyboard / Makey Makey learning: little key-caps along the bottom of Commando Run, Jump Over and Star Blaster light up in the same colours
    as the piano notes whenever a key or pad is pressed. In the first 3 stages of Commando Run, key pictures pop up above the soldier
    (-> to start, up arrow before a pit, Space when an enemy is near). Change KEY_HINT_STAGES in js/commando.js (0 = off).

WHAT CHANGED IN VERSION 2
  - Commando Run: 15 stages, a scrolling world you run through (enemies wait for you - you go and find them), floating platforms to jump
    and stand on (press Down on a platform to drop through), pits to jump (a friendly rescue costs one heart), checkpoint flags,
    hearts to find, a cartoon soldier with a rifle, 7 weapons, 7 enemy types and a different big boss at the end of every stage.
  - Word Hunt is now a WORD SEARCH (6 rounds, getting bigger and trickier).
  - Jump Over!: 12 levels, 12 different scenery themes, new obstacles (rolling ball, hanging swing, tall wall, gaps in the ground),
    patterns that combine them, and stars to collect. Levels take about 35-60 seconds each.
  - Star Blaster: 12 levels, 5 kinds of falling things (new: zig-zagger, splitter), laser pickup, 3 big bosses (levels 4, 8, 12).
  - Much less text: pictures, big round buttons, keys shown as little key-caps.

KEYS
  Session 1: normal keyboard. Session 2 (Makey Makey): only the 5 keys Left / Up / Down / Right / Space.
  Commando Run: Left/Right run, Up jump (hold Up while standing still to aim UP), Down duck (or drop through a platform), Space shoot (hold to keep firing).

THE 7 COMMANDO WEAPONS (pick up the glowing capsules; you keep a weapon until you lose a heart)
  normal (yellow ball)  rapid (orange streaks, twin barrels)  spread (green 3-way)  big (purple, passes through 3 enemies)
  laser (cyan beam, passes through everything)  homing (pink stars that curve toward enemies)  fire (flames, short range).
  The soldier's rifle and the bullet look different for every weapon. New weapons unlock as the stages go on.

WHERE TO CHANGE DIFFICULTY  (each file starts with a block labelled "TEACHER SETTINGS")
  Commando Run  js/commando.js
    COMMANDO_LIVES, COMMANDO_INVINCIBLE_SECONDS, COMMANDO_HITBOX_PAD (bigger = more forgiving), RUN_SPEED, JUMP_POWER, GRAVITY,
    ORB_SPEED, TURRET_WARNING_SECONDS, DROP_CHANCE, BOSS_HP_BASE / BOSS_HP_PER_STAGE (boss toughness),
    WEAPONS (fire rate, damage, how many enemies a shot passes through),
    COMMANDO_LEVELS: one line per stage - len (length), d (0 gentle ... 1 hardest: more and tougher enemies, faster shots),
    theme (scenery), boss and pal (which creature and colours).
    BOSS_DEF: how each boss fights (moves: orbs, fan, rain, wave).
    Every stage is built the same way each time (so replays and checkpoints work); change len/d to make it longer/easier.
  Jump Over!    js/jump.js
    JUMP_LIVES, JUMP_INVINCIBLE_SECONDS, JUMP_HITBOX_PAD, JUMP_STOMP_SLACK (landing on a bush bounces), JUMP_GRAVITY / JUMP_POWER,
    JUMP_LEVELS: speed, count (how many obstacle groups), gap (seconds between), theme.
    PATTERNS: which obstacle groups exist, from which level (min) and how common (w).
  Star Blaster  js/blaster.js
    BLASTER_LIVES, BLASTER_INVINCIBLE_SECONDS, BLASTER_HITBOX_PAD, SHIP_SPEED, BLASTER_WEAPON_SECONDS, FIRE_DELAY,
    ENEMIES (size/speed/hits), BLASTER_LEVELS: count, gap, types, speedMul, rockHp, boss (hit points).
  Pattern Pop!  js/pattern.js   PATTERN_START_LENGTH, PATTERN_LIT_SECONDS, PATTERN_GAP_SECONDS, PATTERN_GRACE_RETRIES.
  Fruit Slice   js/fruit.js     FRUIT_FALL_SECONDS (BIGGER = SLOWER), FRUIT_LETTERS, FRUIT_SPAWN_SECONDS, FRUIT_MAX_ON_SCREEN.
                                (an endless practice game - it never ends, by design)
  Word Hunt     js/words.js     WORD_LIST (word + picture), ROUNDS (grid size, how many words, which directions).
  Piano         js/piano.js     SONGS, BEATS.

USING YOUR OWN PICTURES LATER
  All drawing is in js/sprites.js and js/sprites_world.js - one function per thing, each taking (ctx, x, y, ...state):
    SP.runner, SP.bush / rockFriend / stump / bee / ball / swing / wall / coin   (Jump Over)
    SP.ship, SP.rockBig / comet / jelly / zig / splitter / megaBoss              (Star Blaster)
    SP.hero (the soldier), SP.walker / hopper / gunner / flyer / bomber / turret / armoured, SP.bossDraw (12 bosses),
    SP.capsule, SP.bullet, SP.platform, SP.ground, SP.pit, SP.world (backgrounds), SP.flag     (Commando Run)
    SP.fruit, SP.pad, SP.heart.
  To swap one, replace its body with ctx.drawImage(...). The game files never draw shapes themselves. (Image loading is not built in.)

HOW IT WAS TESTED  (automated: headless Chrome driven over DevTools, opened from file://; games also run faster-than-real-time by test bots)
  - Commando Run: all 15 stages were played start to finish by a test bot in "can't be hurt" mode: every stage (all 12 boss creatures,
    pits, platforms, checkpoints, every pickup type) reached Level Complete, so nothing blocks progress. With normal hearts a simple bot
    cleared stages 1-8 and lost to some of the harder ones - the difficulty rises steadily. A bug found and fixed: a bomber hovering
    beside the hero could not be hit when aiming up; bombers now centre over the hero.
  - Jump Over!: a perfect-play bot cleared all 12 levels (34-61 s each) with no hearts lost; ducking, pits, rolling balls, swings and
    walls all worked. Earlier checks: key-mashing, doing nothing -> gentle "Nice try", one key restarts.
  - Star Blaster: a simple bot cleared levels 1-7, 11 and failed some of 8, 9, 10 and 12 (including 2 of the 3 bosses) - so it is a real challenge.
  - Word Hunt: dragged across a hidden word with the mouse -> word found and ticked, finished the round -> "Round done" -> next round.
  - Fruit Slice, Pattern Pop, Piano (including a real, non-silent recording and no microphone prompt) re-checked after the changes.
  - Layout: stage stays 16:9 with no gap and no scrolling at 700x900, 1280x800, 1920x1080 (and the smaller sizes checked before).
  - Searched for localStorage, cookies, fetch, XMLHttpRequest, http(s):// and external links: none (only css/style.css is linked).

WHAT I COULD NOT CHECK / SIMPLIFIED
  - I could not listen to the sound (no speakers in the test setup) - sounds are triggered and the recording has audio, but please
    give the volume a listen. Only tested in Chrome, with simulated key presses; not on a real Makey Makey.
  - "Challenging" is a judgement call: I tested with bots, not children. If it is too hard or too easy, change the numbers above
    (COMMANDO_LEVELS d / len, COMMANDO_LIVES, BOSS_HP_*, JUMP_LEVELS speed, BLASTER_LEVELS gap/speedMul).
  - A level picker (small numbered circles) is on every level's start screen, so you can jump to any stage; to lock levels you would
    need to remove that (and nothing is saved between visits - by design, no storage is used).
  - Functions starting with an underscore (e.g. CC.modes.commando._sim) are test hooks only.
  - I overwrote your old index.html (the previous piano page) at the start of version 1; it was not in files.zip so it could not be restored.
